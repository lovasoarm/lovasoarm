## Current work

### [ARCHITECTE-FANTOME](https://github.com/lovasoarm/ARCHITECTE-FANTOME)

ARCHITECTE-FANTOME (AF) is the engineering curriculum I am building and following. It starts with programming fundamentals and moves toward software engineering, backend systems, databases, architecture, cloud and infrastructure, reliability, security, data engineering, machine learning, AI engineering, and technical leadership.

I use AF to keep the learning sequence clear and to connect each subject to practice and evidence. The goal is not to count completed lessons. It is to become able to build a system, explain its behaviour, test its limits, diagnose failures, and justify engineering decisions.

## Long-term direction

My long-term direction is **Software and Systems Architecture**, including systems that use AI. I am working toward the scope associated with Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer, and, much later, a Distinguished-level individual contributor.

These are career targets, not titles I claim today. The work behind them requires sound technical judgment, ownership of systems, architecture, reliability, security, cost awareness, clear communication, and influence across teams. I am building toward that scope from the fundamentals, with backend and systems engineering as a strong base.

AI is part of this direction, but it does not replace the need to understand the software, data, infrastructure, risks, and operating conditions around a model.

## Current learning areas

- **Software engineering:** programming, data structures, algorithms, testing, debugging, and maintainable code.
- **Backend and data:** APIs, databases, SQL, data modelling, and transactions.
- **Systems and infrastructure:** Linux, networking, cloud, containers, delivery workflows, and observability.
- **Architecture and reliability:** system design, security, failure handling, performance, and technical trade-offs.

- **Technical communication:** writing, explaining decisions, and reading engineering documentation in English.

## How I work through a problem

```text
Understand the problem
        |
Inspect constraints and assumptions
        |
Build a testable solution
        |
Test normal and failure paths
        |
Measure actual behaviour
        |
Document decisions and trade-offs
        |
Review the result and improve it
```

A few principles guide my work:

- Fundamentals before abstractions.
- Evidence before claims.
- Measure before optimizing.
- Security and reliability belong in the design, not at the end.
- Tools should follow constraints, not trends.
- AI-generated code must be read, tested, reviewed, and understood before I rely on it.
